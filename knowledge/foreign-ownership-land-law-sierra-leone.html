Foreign Ownership and Land Law

Updated 2026-10-14

This is the point on which foreign transactions most often come unstuck, and it requires current legal advice rather than a summary. The principle is that non-citizens do not enjoy the same rights to hold land as citizens. Freehold ownership is generally not available to non-citizens.

Foreign investors can take leases for terms of not more than 55 years, with an option to renew for a further 21 years in most cases. In the provinces, where customary tenure applies, the lease term is capped at 50 years. For mining and large agricultural investments, leases of up to 99 years are possible. Foreign land-based investments must reserve at least 5% of shares for Sierra Leoneans.

The Customary Land Rights Act 2022 (No. 20 of 2022) governs land subject to customary law. It protects customary land rights, eliminates discrimination under customary law, guarantees equal rights for women, and grants communities the right to free, prior and informed consent over industrial activity on their territory. The National Land Commission Act 2022 (No. 19 of 2022) establishes the Commission and District Land Commissions to administer land nationally.

Do not buy anything across a border without a Sierra Leonean lawyer who does this work routinely — not a generalist, not a friend of the seller. The structure of the holding is as important as the land itself, and a badly structured purchase can be unwound years later.

§ Statutes

Customary Land Rights Act
No. 20 of 2022
PurposeProtects customary land rights, eliminates discrimination under customary law, and governs the administration of customary land. Divided into ten Parts and 48 articles.
WomenGuarantees equal rights in land ownership and mandates joint registration for married or cohabiting individuals.
ConsentGrants communities the right to free, prior and informed consent over industrial activity on their territory.
Non-citizensLeasehold not to exceed 50 years in the provinces. At least 5% of shares in any foreign land-based investment reserved for Sierra Leoneans.
National Land Commission Act
No. 19 of 2022
PurposeEstablishes the National Land Commission and District Land Commissions to secure effective and holistic land administration. 91 articles in thirteen Parts.
Communal landDefines communal land as any land subject to customary law, including wetlands, forests, grazing lands and market areas.
Dispute resolutionThe Commission has authority to regulate all conduct relating to land, including dispute resolution.
Non-Citizens (Interests in Land) Act
No. 30 of 1966
Western AreaProhibits non-citizens from purchasing freehold land in the Western Area without a licence from the Board established under the Act. In practice, non-citizens take leases.
Reserved leaseholdRestricts acquisition of reserved leasehold land without a licence.
National Investment Board Act
No. 11 of 2022
Foreign investmentThe NIB facilitates and registers foreign investment. Local content requirements apply.
RestrictionsThe government imposes caps on foreign ownership in mining, fisheries and banking.
ProtectionProvides for free transfer of funds and protection against expropriation.
